The cautious climb is priced, not counted. The steady-state upshift streak
becomes `U(k) = max(ceil(B / G), ceil(((R_{k+1} - R_k) / R_k) / rho))`: at
least one subscriber-buffer-depth (`B = subscriber_buffer_ms`, `G` the
observed group cadence) of clean groups, and more across ladder rungs whose
bitrates are far apart, scaled by the new probing risk tolerance
`wasd.rho` in `(0, 1]` (default 1.0 — the written behaviour on the sample
ladder). A subscriber may signal its own tolerance as a trailing
percentage (1..=100) in the set's algorithm parameters. Connections with
no negotiated buffer or no observed cadence yet keep the written default
streak of five.
